SOLEN
Client Logo Package
Selected direction: Option 3, Planetary Ring

Recommended direction:
The Planetary Ring concept is the stronger choice for a satellite and space technology identity.
The orbital ring directly communicates space, satellites, trajectory and connectivity while keeping
the SOLEN wordmark distinctive and usable across digital and physical applications.

Concept:
The letter O becomes an orbital planet form. The ring suggests satellite orbit and continuous motion.
The warm solar gold accent adds a premium technology feel against deep space blue.

Primary palette:
Solar Gold: #F4A62A
Space Blue: #0B1A2B
Orbit Slate: #2E3A59
Light: #F7F9FC

Typography:
Montserrat

3. Monochrome presentation
4. Logo variation strip

6. Client preview PDF

These are presentation ready concept exports. For final production, the selected mark should be
